about me

I have been interested in coding since the age of 10.

I started my journey with web development, learning HTML and CSS, and later expanded my skills by using JavaScript to build more dynamic and functional websites. As my curiosity grew, I moved into backend development and Python, gaining a deeper understanding of how systems work behind the scenes.

Over the past few years, I have been actively working in game development. I began with Unity and later participated in the GamePons Game Jam. After being selected, I joined their incubation program, where, as part of iSD Studio, I learned Unreal Engine and project management with the support of experienced mentors.

Currently, I am working on Ranch Days, focusing on developing engaging gameplay while continuously improving my technical and creative skills.
